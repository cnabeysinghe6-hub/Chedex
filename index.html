About Chedex

A studio obsessed with how the web feels.

Good sites load fast, read clearly and leave a small spark behind.

The Chedex mark is a sun rising inside a "C": a bright centre, with movement around it. That is how we build websites. One clear idea in the middle, and motion that guides people toward it.

We are a web development agency that works across UI/UX design, animation and front-end engineering. Small projects and big launches get the same care.

How we work

Four things we won't compromise on.

Clarity first

If a visitor can't tell what you do in five seconds, the animation doesn't matter.

Motion with purpose

Every animation guides attention or confirms an action. Nothing moves just to move.

Built to last

Clean code, fast pages and a setup your team can edit without calling us.

Honest scope

Fixed plans, clear timelines and straight answers when something won't work.
